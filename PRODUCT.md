# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Therapy clients & reflective individuals looking for a therapeutic journaling assistant. The context is self-reflection, emotional processing, and chat with an AI companion. The job is to write notes, receive guidance/feedback, and explore personal thoughts in a safe space.

## Product Purpose
Provide a calming, secure, and warm therapeutic journaling experience. It integrates note taking (Tiptap) with an Argentine Spanish AI therapist companion that can reference notes and propose edits, enabling real-time therapeutic support.

## Brand Personality
Calming, Warm, Safe, Intimate. It should feel like a premium personal journal rather than a clinical medical tool or a productivity application.

## Anti-references
Clinical/cold medical aesthetics, generic SaaS UI templates (e.g., ghost-card, gray text, over-rounded card grids), loud or distracting animations, side-stripe borders on card list items.

## Design Principles
- **Intimate Safety**: The UI should feel like a private physical journal (warm off-white, elegant typography like Outfit/Lora, calm teal accents).
- **Unobtrusive Companionship**: The AI therapist panel and chat should feel supportive and integrated, never interrupting the writing flow.
- **Low Cognitive Load**: Clear layouts, no unnecessary decoration or complex grids, allowing full focus on writing and reflection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast text (at least 4.5:1).
- Readable font sizes with Outfit/Lora.
- Support for reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`).
- Clear visual focus indicators.
